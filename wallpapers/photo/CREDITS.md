# Photo wallpapers — credits

Real photographs used by the Script Test profiles' **photo** look variant
(`presets/profile/st-*.variants/photo.conf`). Every one is released as **CC0**
or is in the **public domain** on Wikimedia Commons, so they ship with the
repository; no attribution is required, but the photographers are credited
here. Images are center-cropped and scaled to 1920×1080.

The machine-readable list is `sources.json` (python/hyprsetup/photos.py).
