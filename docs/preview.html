Widgets

Battery Status

battery

A small panel showing the charge percentage and whether it is charging (eww or Quickshell — depending on your Widget Engine choice). Shows empty on desktop systems without a battery.

Screen Brightness

brightness

Shows the current screen brightness percentage via brightnessctl (Quickshell). Requires the brightness component.

Calendar

calendar

A monthly calendar in the top-right corner of the screen (eww or Quickshell — depending on your Widget Engine choice; both use the built-in calendar component and need no extra dependency).

Digital Clock

clock

A large clock and date widget in the top-right corner of the screen (eww or Quickshell — depending on your Widget Engine choice).

Disk Usage

disk

Shows how full the "/" partition is, as a percentage (Quickshell).

System Info Card

fetch-mini

A small fastfetch-like card showing the hostname, kernel version and shell (Quickshell).

Keyboard Layout

kblayout

Shows the active keyboard layout via hyprctl (Quickshell).

Now Playing

music

Shows the current track's artist/title via playerctl (eww or Quickshell — depending on your Widget Engine choice). Shows empty when no player is running. Note: requires the audio-tools component (playerctl).

Network Status

network

Shows the name of the active network connection (nmcli; eww or Quickshell — depending on your Widget Engine choice). Shows "Not connected" when offline. Note: requires the networkmanager component.

System Info

sysinfo

A small panel showing CPU and RAM usage (eww or Quickshell — depending on your Widget Engine choice).

Temperature

temperature

Shows the CPU temperature via /sys/class/thermal (Quickshell). May vary by hardware; needs no extra package.

Uptime

uptime

Shows how long the system has been up (Quickshell).

Volume Level

volume

Shows the current volume level and mute state via pamixer (Quickshell). Requires the audio-tools component.

Workspace Indicator

workspace

Shows the name of the currently focused workspace via hyprctl (Quickshell) — an extra reminder, independent of the status bar.

World Clocks

worldclock

Shows the time in London, Tokyo and New York (Quickshell). Uses the system's own tzdata; needs no extra package.

Bar thickness is drawn at its real proportion of the screen; thin bars are exaggerated 2x so they do not vanish, but since the exaggeration applies equally to both axes, horizontal bars and vertical rails stay comparable. The wallpaper is the profile's own generated image; fonts and blur are not in the mockups, and the real look changes with them.
